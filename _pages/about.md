<h1 id="about-me">🤗 About Me</h1>

Hi! I am **Yitong Luo (罗亦彤)**, an undergraduate at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/), pursuing dual bachelor's degrees in **Chemical Biology and Biomedical Engineering**. I conduct research at **BirthLab**, under the supervision of **Prof. Qiyuan Tian** at the School of Biomedical Engineering, Tsinghua University.

I will pursue a master's degree in **Electronic Information (Artificial Intelligence)** at **Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School**, with expected study dates of September 2027 to June 2029.

My research interests include **medical AI** and **generalizable, trustworthy AI**.
